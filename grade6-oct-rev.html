Correct answer: C. 6 = 2 × 3 and 9 = 3 × 3. GCF = 3. LCM = 2 × 3 × 3 = 18.

Correct answer: B. The largest number of equal groups is the GCF of 18 and 12, which is 6 (each group gets 3 bags of rice and 2 bottles of oil).
